How these numbers are calculated
  • Everything comes from your TallyPrime company through a read-only connection on this computer. Nothing is written back to Tally, and the data never leaves this computer.
  • Money to collect / we owe use party ledger balances. Bill-wise detail is only used to work out the age, matching each balance to the newest open bills first.
  • Billed / collected are taken from each Sales and Receipt voucher's customer line, incl. GST.
  • Profit uses ledger balances under Sales, Purchase, Direct and Indirect groups plus stock valuation at the start of the year and today.
  • Government customers are recognised by name (Government, Department, Directorate, Municipal…).
  • This page refreshes itself every 5 minutes. The sync program pulls fresh data from Tally every 15 minutes.
